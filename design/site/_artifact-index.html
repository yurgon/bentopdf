Design foundation

One typeface in three weights, a white page with warm-grey neutrals, and one honey-amber accent kept for the main action. Every page is built from the parts below. Switch the theme in the top right to see the dark version.

Variant A · Calm

Light from top to bottom. The tool sits alone in the middle of a white page, the long text has an outline on the left.

Variant B · Stage

A dark band holds the title and the tool side by side, like a product screen. The reading part below is light.

Color

Neutrals carry a trace of the accent’s hue so the greys feel chosen. Amber appears only on the main button, the current selection and progress. Body text is at least 4.5:1 in both themes.

Type

Hanken Grotesk, an open-source (OFL) grotesque, in regular, medium and semibold. Fixed sizes for the interface, a slightly larger size and wider leading for long text.
