A signature generator turns your name or your hand into a clean picture of a signature: dark ink, no white box around it, ready to drop into a document. Every picture on this page is made inside your browser. Your name, drawing and photo are not sent anywhere and are gone when you close the tab.

Type your name and pick a handwriting style

Type your name the way you sign it: full name, first initial and surname, or just initials. Every handwriting style shows your own name at once, so you can compare them side by side instead of clicking through one font at a time. There are fourteen styles, from neat and readable (Dancing Script, Parisienne) to fast and loose like a real autograph (Mrs Saint Delafield, Herr Von Muellerhoff).

A typed signature looks tidy, but it is the same for anyone who types the same name. If the signature should look like yours, draw it or use a photo.

Draw your signature with a finger or mouse

On a phone, sign in the box with your finger, the way you sign for a delivery. Turn the phone sideways for more room. On a computer, a trackpad or a drawing tablet works better than a mouse.

The line is smoothed as you draw and gets a little thinner where your hand moves fast, like a real pen. Undo removes the last stroke only, so a slip on the final letter does not cost you the whole signature.

Turn a photo of your paper signature into a transparent PNG

This gives the most natural result, because it is your real signature. For a clean image:

  • Sign on plain white paper with a dark pen. A felt tip works best.
  • Take the photo in daylight or under a bright lamp, holding the phone straight above the paper so your hand does not cast a shadow.
  • Fill most of the photo with the signature.

The page evens out shadows and the grey tint of the paper, keeps only the ink and removes dust and specks. If parts of the line disappear, move “Clean-up” to the left. If grey patches remain, move it to the right.

Sign a PDF with your new signature

Choose “Sign a PDF with it” and open the document. It opens on the last page with the signature already placed near the bottom right, where most documents are signed. Drag it onto the line, pull the corner to make it larger or smaller, and add more copies with “Add to this page” if you need to sign several pages. Then save the signed PDF.

The signature becomes part of the page, so it looks the same in every PDF reader and prints exactly where you put it. To fill in form fields or add the date next to it, use Sign PDF with form fields as well.

How it works

A typed signature is drawn from the font at a large size, then thickened or slanted. A drawn signature is stored as the path of your strokes and redrawn at high resolution, so it never looks pixelated. For a photo, the page estimates the brightness of the paper in each area, compares every pixel with it to find the ink, picks the cut-off between ink and paper automatically, and drops tiny blobs that cannot be part of a signature.

In every case the result is cropped to the ink, coloured and given a transparent background. The SVG is not a picture wrapped in a vector file: the outline of the ink is traced into smooth curves. All of this runs in JavaScript on your device, which is why it also works with no connection once the page has loaded.

Is an image signature legally valid?

This page makes a picture of a signature. It is not a certified electronic signature, and it does not prove who placed it on a document. Whether it is accepted depends on the document and the country.

Many countries, including the United States and the members of the European Union, do not reject a signature only because it is electronic, so a signature image is often fine for everyday paperwork: a lease, a school form, a delivery note, an invoice. Some documents need more, such as a notarised signature, a qualified electronic signature from a certified provider, or a signature in ink. Wills, property deeds and some court and tax papers often fall into this group. If you are not sure, ask whoever asked you to sign. Nothing on this page is legal advice.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

  • Signing a contract, lease or permission form sent as a PDF.
  • Adding a signature to a letter or invoice written in Word or Google Docs.
  • A signature at the bottom of an email.
  • A signature for a stamp, a certificate or a printed card.

It will not help when a document must carry a digital certificate (for that, see digital signatures with a certificate), or when the receiver asks you to sign in their own signing service. A PDF that is locked against changes has to be unlocked first.

How to make your signature look good

A signature reads better when the first letter is clear and the rest flows. Keep it about as wide as five or six letters of normal handwriting, give it a slight lean to the right, and avoid too many loops: they turn into blots when the image is made small. Blue ink makes it obvious on a printout that the page was signed, not copied.

Questions and answers

Is this signature generator free?
Yes. There is no account, no trial and no watermark, and every download is available to everyone. The page costs almost nothing to run because the work happens on your device.
Do you store my signature?
No. Your name, drawing and photo never leave your browser, and the page does not save them anywhere. Close the tab and they are gone. Keep the PNG or SVG you downloaded if you want to use it again.
How do I get a signature with a transparent background?
Every PNG and SVG from this page already has a transparent background: only the ink is there. The checkered pattern behind the preview shows the see-through area. For a paper signature, use the Photo option and the paper is removed for you.
Can I use this signature in Word, Google Docs or a PDF?
Yes. In Word, use Insert, Pictures and set the wrapping to “In front of text” to move it freely. In Google Docs, use Insert, Image. For a PDF, choose “Sign a PDF with it” on this page.
Is a signature image the same as an electronic signature?
Not quite. An image of a signature is one simple kind of electronic signature. A certified (digital or qualified) electronic signature also records who signed and proves the document was not changed afterwards. This page does not create that kind.
Is a typed or drawn signature legally binding?
It depends on the document and the country. Many everyday agreements accept it, while wills, deeds and some official forms require a notarised, certified or ink signature. Ask the person who sent you the document if you are unsure. This is not legal advice.
How do I make my signature look more professional?
Make the first letter clear, keep the rest smooth, lean it slightly to the right and avoid tiny loops. Draw it a few times and keep the best one, or photograph your real signature for the most natural look.
Can I create initials as well?
Yes. Type your initials instead of your full name, or draw them, and download them the same way. Many contracts ask for initials on every page: in the signing step, add one copy to each page.
