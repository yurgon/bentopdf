How to print a printer test page

  1. Pick the paper

    Choose A4 or Letter, whichever is in the printer tray. The page picks the usual size for your country.

  2. Choose the sheets

    Black and white and Color are ticked. Add Alignment to check scale and margins, Two-sided to see how your printer flips paper.

  3. Press Print

    Your browser’s print dialog opens. Choose the printer and set the size to Actual size or 100%, not Fit to page.

  4. Read the result

    Compare the sheet with the checks below: even black, all gray steps, four clean ink bars, a ruler that measures 100 mm.

What you get

A printer test page is a sheet built to show printing faults at a glance: solid areas reveal streaks, fine lines and small text show sharpness, color bars show which cartridge is empty, and a ruler shows whether the page was scaled. Print one here in a click. Nothing is uploaded and no printer software is needed.

The four sheets

Black and white test page

A full-width block of solid black, eleven gray steps from 0 to 100%, a smooth gradient, lines from 0.1 to 4 points wide, text from 4 to 16 points, a fine checkerboard and a circle. Use it for laser printers and to check the black cartridge of an inkjet.

Color test page with CMYK bars and gradients

Solid bars of cyan, magenta, yellow and black, the mixed colors they make (red, green, blue, orange, purple, teal), tint ramps of each ink from 10 to 100%, a full spectrum, and two gray strips: one mixed from the color inks, one from black ink alone. In the PDF the bars are pure CMYK, so each bar asks for one ink only.

Alignment and margins test

Frames on the paper edge and 3, 5 and 10 mm inside it, a 100 mm ruler across and another down the side, a grid of 10 mm squares with diagonals, and a center mark. It tells you whether the page was scaled, how much of the edge your printer cannot reach, and whether the paper feeds straight.

Double-sided (duplex) print test

A front and a back page with big arrows and the words TOP EDGE. Print both on one sheet with the settings you plan to use, then hold it up to a window: both arrows should point to the same edge. It is the same sheet the PDF booklet maker offers, because a booklet printed with the wrong flip comes out upside down on every other page.

How to read your test page

What a good print looks like and what each fault means
Look at Good print If not
Solid black block Even and deep, no white lines White lines across: clogged nozzles or a worn drum
Gray steps All 11 steps look different Light steps vanish: toner or ink is low; dark steps merge: too much ink
Ink bars Four clean bars in the right colors A bar missing or wrong: that cartridge is empty, dried or not seated
Thin lines and small text 0.1 pt line and 4 pt text visible Blurry or doubled: print head needs alignment
100 mm ruler Measures 100 mm Shorter: the print dialog scaled the page
Edge frames Inner frames complete Outer frames cut off: that is your printer’s margin, normal

Streaks, faded or missing colors: what to do

  • Horizontal white lines in inkjet prints. Run the head cleaning cycle from the printer’s menu or its settings app, then print the test again. Two or three cycles often clear a clogged nozzle; more than that wastes ink.
  • One color missing or wrong. Check that cartridge is not empty and is clicked into place. A cartridge that has sat unused for months may need several cleanings or a replacement.
  • Vertical streaks or smudges on laser prints. Usually the drum or toner cartridge. Gently shake the toner cartridge and wipe the glass of the scanner if the fault also shows on copies.
  • Doubled or jagged lines. Run print head alignment from the printer’s maintenance menu.
  • Everything slightly too small. The page was printed with Fit to page. Choose Actual size or 100% in the print dialog.
  • Squares come out as rectangles or the page is crooked. Straighten the paper stack, push the guides against it and use fresh, flat paper.

How to print the built-in test page in Windows and macOS

Every printer also has its own test page, which shows the driver’s settings and the nozzle pattern. In Windows 11, open Settings, then Bluetooth and devices, Printers and scanners, pick your printer and choose Print test page. In macOS, open System Settings, Printers and Scanners, select the printer, open the print queue and use the Printer menu. Many printers can also print one from their own buttons, without a computer: look for a report or maintenance menu on the printer’s screen.

The built-in page is made for the service technician. The sheets on this page are made to be read by anyone and work with every printer brand the same way.

How it works

The sheets are drawn in your browser as vector graphics, so lines and text are as sharp as your printer can make them. Print sends them to your browser’s print dialog at real size; Download PDF saves the same sheets as a PDF in which the ink bars and tints are defined in CMYK. Nothing is uploaded and the page keeps working offline once loaded.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Checking a printer that has not been used for weeks, deciding whether to buy a new cartridge, testing a new or second-hand printer, setting up two-sided printing before a booklet, checking that a print shop or office printer does not shrink pages.

  • It does not measure color accuracy. Screens and printers show color differently; for exact color you need a calibrated printer profile.
  • Photo printers with six or more inks show the extra inks only as part of the mixed colors.
  • It cannot fix the printer. It shows what is wrong; cleaning, alignment and cartridges are done from the printer itself.

Questions and answers

What is a printer test page?
A sheet designed to show printing faults: solid areas for streaks, fine lines and small text for sharpness, color bars for empty cartridges and a ruler for scaling. Print one here, or the printer’s own built-in page from its settings.
Why print a test page?
To find out whether a problem comes from the printer or from the document, to check ink before an important print, and to make sure a printer that sat unused still prints cleanly.
What should a printer test page look like?
Even, deep black with no white lines, eleven distinct gray steps, four clean ink bars in the right colors, a visible 0.1 point line and a ruler that measures exactly 100 mm.
Why does my test page have lines or streaks?
White lines across inkjet prints come from clogged nozzles: run the head cleaning cycle. Streaks down laser prints usually come from the drum or toner. The table above lists each fault.
Why is one color missing?
That cartridge is empty, dried out or not seated. Check the ink level and that the cartridge clicks into place, then run a head cleaning and print the color sheet again.
Can I print a test page without a computer?
Most printers can print their own test or status page from the menu on the printer screen or by holding a button. This page needs a computer or phone with a browser.
Does a test page use a lot of ink?
A little. The black and white sheet uses about as much black as a page of photos. Print only the sheets you need: the color sheet when you suspect a color, alignment when sizes matter.
Which paper size should I choose: A4 or Letter?
The size in your printer tray. Letter is standard in the United States and Canada, A4 almost everywhere else. The page picks one for your region; switch it if your tray holds the other.
