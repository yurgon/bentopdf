How to delete pages from a PDF

  1. Add your PDF

    Every page is shown as a thumbnail, with the total count.

  2. Mark the pages to remove

    Click them on the thumbnails, or type them: commas between pages, a hyphen for ranges, such as 2, 4-6, 9.

  3. Delete and save

    The PDF without those pages is saved to your device. Your original is not changed.

What you get

Deleting pages from a PDF takes out the ones you do not want, such as a blank scan, an outdated appendix or a cover nobody needs, and keeps the rest exactly as it was. Here you do it in your browser: no upload, no account, no limit on file size.

Remove pages that are not next to each other

Click each page you want gone on the thumbnails, or type them all in one line: 2, 4-6, 9 removes five pages in one go. To also reorder or rotate pages, use Organize PDF.

Delete blank pages

Scanners often add empty backs of single-sided sheets. Type their numbers here, or let Remove Blank Pages find them for you in a long scan.

Links, bookmarks and file size

The pages you keep are copied as they are: text stays searchable, links to websites keep working and nothing is recompressed. Bookmarks are not carried over to the new file, so if your PDF has a bookmark list, add it again afterwards. The file usually gets smaller by roughly the share of pages you removed, more if those pages held photos.

How it works

The PDF is opened in your browser, and a new PDF is built from the pages you did not list, in their original order, by a PDF library running on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere, and the result is saved straight to your device.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Removing a cover sheet or a blank page from a scan, dropping private pages before sharing a statement, cutting an old section from a manual, or trimming a file to fit an upload limit.

  • Deleting a page does not remove it from your original. Keep the original if you may need the pages again.
  • To hide part of a page, not the whole page, use Redact PDF.
  • Password-protected PDFs need the password first.
  • Signed PDFs lose their digital signature when pages are removed, because the document changes.

Questions and answers

How do I delete pages from a PDF?
Add the file, click the pages to remove or type them, such as 2, 4-6, and press Delete Pages. The new PDF is saved to your device.
How can I remove unwanted pages for free?
On this page it is free, with no account, no watermark and no limit on size.
Can I delete pages that are not next to each other?
Yes. Click each one, or type them separated by commas, for example 2, 7, 11-13.
Why can't I delete a page in my PDF?
Usually the file is password protected or you are in a viewer that cannot edit. Open it here; if it asks for a password, enter it, or remove it with Unlock PDF.
Does removing pages make the file smaller?
Yes, usually by about the share of pages removed, and more if they had photos.
Will bookmarks be kept?
No. The new file is built from the pages you keep, and the bookmark list is not copied. Text and web links are kept.
Is my file uploaded?
No. The pages are removed in your browser and the file never leaves your device.
