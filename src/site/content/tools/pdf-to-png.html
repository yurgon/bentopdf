How to convert a PDF to PNG

  1. Add the PDF

    Choose one PDF file. Locked files ask for their password.

  2. Choose the size

    2x is sharp on any screen. Use 3x for printing or zooming in, 1x for small previews.

  3. Convert and save

    Press Convert. You get one PNG per page; several pages come as a ZIP.

PDF to PNG turns pages into lossless pictures: no blur around letters, no blotches on flat colours. That makes PNG the right choice for charts, diagrams, screenshots of text and anything you will zoom into. The pages are drawn in your browser, and nothing is sent to a server.

Which scale to choose

A PDF page has no pixels of its own, so you choose how big the image should be. The scale multiplies the page's normal screen size of 72 pixels per inch. 2x suits screens, slides and documents. 3x gives 216 pixels per inch, good for printing a page at its original size or for cutting out a detail. Higher scales make larger files: a full A4 page at 3x can be several megabytes.

Is it better to convert PDF to JPG or PNG?

PNG keeps every pixel exactly, so text, thin lines and solid colours stay crisp; files are larger. JPG throws away detail you rarely notice in photos and makes much smaller files. Pages that are mostly text or graphics: PNG. Pages that are mostly photos or scans: PDF to JPG.

Convert PDF to PNG on iPhone and Android

Open this page in Safari or Chrome on the phone, tap the upload area and choose the PDF from Files or Downloads. A single page is saved as one PNG; to put it into Photos on an iPhone, open it in Files and choose Save Image. Several pages arrive as a ZIP, which Files opens with a tap.

How it works

Each page is drawn onto a canvas in your browser at the scale you chose, exactly as a PDF viewer draws it, and saved as a PNG. Pages are drawn on white, as they look on screen. Everything happens on your device, so even confidential documents are safe to convert.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Charts and diagrams for slides, pages for a wiki or a help article, a page of sheet music to annotate on a tablet, a design proof to send for review, a thumbnail for a website.

  • Transparent backgrounds are not kept; pages come out on white. For a logo with transparency, PDF to SVG keeps it as a vector.
  • One PDF at a time. Convert several files one after another.
  • Text in a PNG can no longer be searched or copied.
