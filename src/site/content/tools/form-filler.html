How to fill out a PDF form

  1. Open the form

    Add the PDF. It opens in a viewer on the page and stays on your device.

  2. Fill it in

    Click a field and type, tick checkboxes, pick from dropdowns. If the form is flat, use the Text tool in the viewer toolbar and click where the answer goes.

  3. Save the filled form

    Press Save & Download Filled Form to get a normal PDF with your answers inside.

To fill in a PDF form is to type your answers into its fields and save the result, so you can send or print the finished form. Many forms are real fillable PDFs; others are only a picture of a form. This page handles both, in your browser, so the form and your personal details are never uploaded.

Fill a fillable PDF form

A fillable form has real fields. They become live inputs in the viewer: type into text boxes, tick checkboxes, choose from dropdowns and radio buttons. When you press Save & Download Filled Form the answers are stored in the fields themselves, so they show up in other PDF viewers too, not only here.

Fill a flat form that has no fields

A form that is only a scan or a printout has nothing to click. Use the Text tool in the viewer toolbar, click where the answer belongs and type. You can also draw with the pen or add an image, such as a picture of your signature, then download the PDF. If you fill the same form often, Create PDF Form can add real fields to it once.

XFA forms

XFA is an older kind of form used by some government and bank documents. Chrome and many online tools show them as blank pages. This form filler can open and fill them, as Firefox can.

Sign and lock the form

This page fills fields and adds text, drawings and images. To add a proper signature, use Sign PDF. To stop anyone changing your answers afterwards, finish with Flatten PDF, which turns the answers into part of the page.

How it works

The form opens in a PDF viewer running inside the page. Your answers are written into the PDF's fields or added as text and drawings on the page, and the file is saved on your device. The form, your answers and personal details never leave your browser.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Job and rental applications, tax and benefit forms, school and medical forms, permission slips, contracts with blanks to complete.

  • Fill first, flatten last. A flattened form can no longer be edited or filled.
  • Check the result in the viewer before you save, so a long answer does not run past its box.
  • Official portals sometimes require their own software for digitally signed forms; this page saves an ordinary PDF.

Questions and answers

How do I fill out a PDF form online?
Open the PDF here, click the fields and type, tick or choose, then press Save & Download Filled Form.
How do I fill a PDF that won't let me type?
If it has no real fields, use the Text tool in the viewer toolbar and click where the answer goes. It adds text anywhere on the page.
Can I fill a PDF form for free?
Yes, with no account, no watermark and no limit on forms.
Can I fill XFA forms?
Yes. XFA forms often look blank in Chrome and other tools, but they open and fill here.
Will my answers show in other PDF viewers?
For fillable forms, yes: the values are stored in the fields. Text you add to a flat form becomes part of the page.
Can I sign the form after filling it?
This page fills fields only. Use Sign PDF for your signature, then Flatten PDF if you want to lock the answers.
Is it safe to fill a form with personal details here?
Yes. The form is filled in your browser and nothing is uploaded, so your details stay on your device.
Can I save my progress and finish later?
Save the PDF with the answers so far, then open that file here again later.
