How to make a crossword puzzle

  1. Type words and clues

    One pair per line: the answer, a dash, then the clue. Or paste two columns from a spreadsheet.

  2. Let the grid build itself

    The words cross on shared letters and the squares are numbered for Across and Down.

  3. Rebuild until you like it

    Press Rebuild grid for a different layout of the same words.

  4. Print the puzzle and the key

    Print the puzzle sheet and the answer key, or save both in one PDF.

What you get

This crossword puzzle maker turns your own words and clues into a printable puzzle: the grid builds itself, words cross on shared letters, squares are numbered, and a separate answer key comes with it. It is free, needs no account and works in your browser, so your word list is never uploaded.

Words and clues

Type one pair per line: the answer first, then a dash, a colon or a tab, then the clue. orbit - The path a planet takes around the Sun works, and so do two columns pasted from Excel or Google Sheets. Answers can be one word or several: spaces and hyphens are left out of the grid, and the clue gets the word lengths in brackets, such as (3, 5) for “ice cream”.

Ten to twenty words make a good classroom puzzle. Words that share common letters (E, A, R, S, T) cross more easily, and a mix of long and short words gives a tighter grid. A word with no clue gets an empty line after its number, so you can write a clue by hand.

How the grid is built

The crossword generator places the longest word first and then fits each next word across an existing one where a letter matches. A word may only touch others where it crosses them, the same rule printed crosswords follow, so every square belongs to a real answer. It tries dozens of orders and keeps the grid that places the most words, has the most crossings and is closest to a square. If a word still has no place, the tool names it: press Rebuild grid or swap it for a word with more common letters.

Answer key

The answer key is a separate page with the same grid, numbered the same way and filled in with the answers, so you can print it apart from the puzzles or keep it on screen. Turn it off if you only need the puzzle. The optional word bank lists all answers under the grid, which turns the puzzle into a matching exercise for younger students.

Crosswords in the classroom

Teachers use crossword puzzles for vocabulary review, spelling lists, science terms, history names and dates, and new words in a foreign language. Write the definition as the clue and the term as the answer, or turn it around: the word in English as the clue and the translation as the answer. A crossword also makes a quiet starter or exit task, and the printed sheet has a name and date line at the top.

How it works

Everything happens in your browser. The grid is laid out on your device, text is measured with the same font used for printing, and Print opens your browser’s print dialog at real size. Download PDF saves the puzzle and the key in one file, on A4 or Letter.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Vocabulary and spelling practice, revision before a test, homeschool lessons, birthday and wedding quizzes about the guests of honor, team events, puzzles for church bulletins and newsletters.

  • Black squares in the style of a newspaper crossword are not drawn: like most classroom puzzles, only the answer squares are printed.
  • Words with no common letters cannot cross. The tool lists any word it could not place.
  • Chinese, Japanese, Korean and emoji characters are not in the print font, so they print as empty boxes. Latin, Cyrillic and Greek letters work.

Questions and answers

Is this crossword puzzle maker free?
Yes. Make, print and download as many crosswords as you like, with no account and no watermark, for class, home or events.
How many words can I use?
There is no fixed limit. Ten to twenty five words fit nicely on one page; with more, the squares get smaller and the clues continue on a second page.
Why did a word not fit in the grid?
A word needs at least one letter in common with a word already placed. Press Rebuild grid to try a different layout, or change the word.
Is there an answer key?
Yes. A separate page shows the same numbered grid with every answer filled in. You can turn it off.
Can I use phrases as answers?
Yes. Spaces and hyphens are dropped in the grid, and the clue shows the length of each word, such as (3, 5).
Can I make a blank crossword puzzle template?
Type the answers without clues: the puzzle sheet then has an empty line after each number, ready for clues written by hand.
Does it work in other languages?
Yes, for languages written with Latin, Cyrillic or Greek letters, including accented letters. Chinese, Japanese and Korean are not supported in print yet.
Is my word list uploaded?
No. The puzzle is made on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
