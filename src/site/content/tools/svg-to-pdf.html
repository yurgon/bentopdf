How to convert SVG to PDF

  1. Add your SVG files

    One file or many. They stay on your device.

  2. Choose the quality

    High keeps a lossless image. Medium and Low give a smaller file.

  3. Convert and save

    Press Convert to PDF. Several SVGs become consecutive pages of one PDF.

SVG is a vector image format used for logos, icons and diagrams. Converting SVG to PDF gives you a page you can print, send or merge with other documents. This page does it in your browser, so your artwork is never uploaded.

Several SVGs in one PDF

Add as many files as you like. They become consecutive pages of one PDF in the order shown in the list, and each page takes the size of its own SVG.

The PDF is a picture, not vector

Each SVG is drawn by your browser and the result is placed on the page as an image. It will not scale without loss, and the text in it is not selectable. If you need true vector output, export the PDF from a vector editor such as Inkscape. To go from PDF to vector, use PDF to SVG.

Page size and resolution

The page matches the SVG's width and height in pixels, one pixel per PDF point. An SVG with only a viewBox renders at 800 by 600, so set explicit dimensions for predictable results. There is no DPI setting; to get more pixels, enlarge the width and height in the SVG. To fit A4 or Letter, use Fix Page Size.

Quality settings

High keeps a lossless PNG at full size. Medium re-encodes as JPEG at 75 percent and limits the longest side to 2500 pixels; Low uses 50 percent and 1500 pixels. For line art and text, High avoids JPEG artifacts.

How it works

Your browser draws each SVG onto a canvas filled with white, and a PDF library on your device puts that image on a page. Nothing is sent to a server.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Logos for letterheads, icons for print sheets, diagrams for reports, designs to share with someone who has no vector software.

  • External resources are not loaded. Embed images as data URIs and convert text to paths before converting.
  • Mix formats with Images to PDF.

Questions and answers

How do I convert SVG to PDF?
Add the SVG files, choose the quality, press Convert to PDF and save.
Is the PDF vector or an image?
An image. The SVG is drawn by the browser and placed on the page, so it does not scale without loss.
What size will the page be?
The SVG's width and height in pixels, one pixel per point. Without them it is 800 by 600.
Can I set 300 DPI?
There is no DPI setting. Enlarge the width and height in the SVG source for more pixels.
Why are text, fonts or images missing?
External files, fonts and CSS cannot load. Embed images as data URIs and convert text to paths.
Is transparency kept?
No. Transparent areas become white.
Can I put several SVGs in one PDF?
Yes. They become consecutive pages in the order of the list.
Is my SVG uploaded?
No. It is converted in your browser and never leaves your device.
