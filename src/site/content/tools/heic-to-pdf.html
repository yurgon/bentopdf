HEIC is the format iPhones save photos in. It keeps photos small, but many Windows computers, websites and upload forms will not open it. Converting HEIC to PDF puts your photos into one document anyone can open. It happens in your browser, so your personal photos are never uploaded.

Several HEIC photos in one PDF

Add every photo at once: receipts for an expense report, pages of a handwritten form, a lease you photographed sheet by sheet. Drag them into order and they become pages of one PDF, which is easier to send and to keep than a pile of separate pictures.

What is a HEIC file?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container, also called HEIF) is the photo format iPhones and iPads use by default since iOS 11. It stores the same picture as a JPG in about half the space. The catch is support: Windows needs an extra codec, and many websites only accept JPG, PNG or PDF. To make an iPhone save JPG instead, go to Settings, Camera, Formats and choose Most Compatible.

Quality and file size

Photos are placed at full resolution without lossy recompression, so nothing is lost, and each page is the size of the photo. The price is a large file: several megabytes per photo. For email or a portal with a size limit, run the result through Compress PDF. To fit photos onto Letter or A4 pages with a quality setting, use Images to PDF, which also takes HEIC.

How it works

Each HEIC photo is decoded in your browser, converted to a lossless image and placed on its own page of a new PDF by a PDF library running on your device. No photo is sent to a server, so it also works on a Windows computer without the HEIC codec and with no app to install.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Receipts and invoices for expenses, photographed documents for a visa or rental application, school work, insurance claims, and sharing iPhone photos with someone on Windows.

  • Files are large because quality is kept; compress them if you need to.
  • Live Photos become still pictures.
  • Text in the photos is not searchable until you run PDF OCR on the result.
  • Many large photos on an older phone can take a while; convert them in smaller groups if the page slows down.

Questions and answers

How do I convert a HEIC file to PDF?
Add the HEIC photos on this page, drag them into order and press Convert to PDF. The PDF is saved to your device.
What is a HEIC file?
It is the photo format iPhones use by default. It is smaller than JPG but does not open everywhere.
Why are my iPhone photos HEIC instead of JPG?
Because the camera is set to High Efficiency. Choose Most Compatible in Settings, Camera, Formats to get JPG.
Can I put several HEIC photos in one PDF?
Yes. Add them all; each becomes one page, in the order you set.
Will quality be reduced?
No. Photos are placed at full resolution. That also makes the PDF large; compress it if needed.
Does it work on Windows?
Yes. It runs in the browser, so no HEIC codec or app is needed.
Are my photos uploaded?
No. They are converted in your browser and never leave your device.
