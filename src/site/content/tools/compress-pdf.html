How to compress a PDF

  1. Add your PDFs

    Choose one file or several. The current size of each is shown.

  2. Choose a level or a size

    Medium suits most files. Or type a target such as 1 MB and the tool finds the best quality that fits.

  3. Compress and save

    Press Compress. The smaller PDF is saved to your device; several files come as a ZIP.

To compress a PDF is to make the file smaller without changing what is on the pages. Most of a PDF's weight is pictures and fonts, so that is where the savings come from. This compressor runs in your browser: your documents are not uploaded, and there is no limit on size or number of files.

Which level to choose

Start with medium. It usually cuts a photo-heavy PDF to a fraction of its size while staying sharp on screen and on an office printer. Choose low when the file will be printed in high quality, high for tight limits, and lossless when nothing may change at all. Custom settings let you fine-tune the compressor, convert pages to greyscale, or turn image-only scans into compact pictures with Rasterize pages.

Compress a PDF to 1 MB, 500 KB or 100 KB

Upload portals often say "maximum 1 MB" or "under 200 KB". Type that number into target size and choose KB or MB: the compressor tries stronger settings until the file fits, and keeps the best quality that does. A text document can usually reach 100 KB; a long scan may not, and the tool tells you if the result is still above the target. Shortcuts for common limits: 100 KB, 200 KB, 500 KB, 1 MB and email.

Why a PDF gets so large

  • Photos at full camera resolution, far more pixels than a page needs.
  • Scans saved as colour images even when the page is black text on white.
  • Whole fonts embedded several times, once for each part that was merged in.
  • Leftover data from editing: old versions of pages, thumbnails and metadata.

A PDF that is only text is usually small already, and compressing it gains little. That is normal, not a fault.

How it works

The compressor recompresses and downsizes images, keeps only the letters each font actually uses, merges duplicate fonts and drops data nothing refers to. Text stays text, so it remains searchable and copyable, and links and form fields keep working. Everything happens in memory on your device and the result is saved straight to it.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Email attachments, job and university applications, government and visa portals, sharing scans in messengers, saving space in cloud storage.

  • Already compressed PDFs shrink little a second time. Try a higher level, or split the file with Split PDF and send it in parts.
  • Password-protected PDFs need the password, which the tool asks for.
  • Digitally signed PDFs are left untouched by default, because any change would break the signature. Compress before signing.

Questions and answers

How do I reduce the size of a PDF?
Add the file on this page, choose medium and press Compress. For a fixed limit, type it into target size, such as 1 MB, and the tool finds the best quality that fits.
How can I reduce a PDF to a certain number of MB?
Enter the number in target size and pick MB or KB. The compressor tries stronger settings until the file is under that size.
Can I compress a PDF without losing quality?
Yes, choose lossless: it removes unused data and duplicate fonts and leaves images untouched. The saving is smaller than with the other levels.
Can I compress a PDF without paying?
Yes. It is free with no account and no watermark, and there is no limit on file size or number of files.
Can I compress a PDF that is already compressed?
You can, but expect a small saving. Try the high level or a target size; if it is still too big, split the PDF into parts.
Are my files uploaded?
No. Compression runs in your browser and the result is saved directly to your device.
Why did my file barely shrink?
It was probably mostly text, which is small already, or it was compressed before. Large savings come from photos and scans.
Will text and links still work?
Yes. Text stays real text, and links and form fields are kept.
