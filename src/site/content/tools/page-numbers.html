How to add page numbers to a PDF

  1. Add your PDF

    One file. It stays on your device.

  2. Choose position and format

    Pick one of six positions, the 1 or 1 / 5 format, the font size and the color.

  3. Add the numbers and save

    Press Add Page Numbers. The numbered PDF downloads.

Page numbers make a long PDF easy to refer to, cite and print. Here you add them in your browser and save a numbered copy, so contracts and reports are never uploaded. The numbers are drawn into the pages and show in every viewer.

Choose where the numbers go

Six positions: bottom center, bottom left, bottom right, top center, top left and top right. The margin adjusts to the page size, about 4 percent of the page between 8 and 40 points, so numbers stay clear of the edge on small and large pages alike. Each page is measured on its own, so mixed Letter and A4 or portrait and landscape pages still get numbers in the visible area.

Page 3 or 3 / 12

Choose 1, 2, 3... for the plain number or 1 / 5, 2 / 5... to show the total. For wording such as "Page 3 of 12", Header & Footer accepts custom text with {page} and {total} placeholders.

Skip a cover or start later

Numbering always starts at 1 on the first page. To leave a cover unnumbered, split it off, number the rest and merge the two again. For Roman numerals on front matter, Add Page Labels sets the numbers viewers display instead of printing them.

The numbers are permanent

They are drawn into the page content, not added as annotations, so every viewer shows them and they print with the document. That also means they cannot be switched off later, so keep your unnumbered original.

How it works

A PDF library in your browser writes each number onto its page in Helvetica at the size and color you chose and saves a new file. The document never leaves your device.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Contracts, reports, theses, scanned bundles, exhibits, manuals and anything you print and want to keep in order.

  • For prefixes such as EXHIBIT-A or legal numbers like ABC000123 use Bates Numbering.
  • The typeface is fixed to Helvetica; only size and color change.

Questions and answers

How do I add page numbers to a PDF?
Add the file, choose the position, format and size, press Add Page Numbers and save.
Where can the numbers go?
In one of six places: bottom or top, at the left, center or right.
Can I show 3 of 12?
Choose the 1 / 5 format and each page prints as 3 / 12. For other wording use Header & Footer.
Can I start numbering later or skip the cover?
Not here. Numbering starts at 1 on the first page. Split the cover off, number the rest and merge again.
Can I change the font?
The typeface is Helvetica. You can set the size from 6 to 72 points and any color.
Are the numbers permanent?
Yes. They are drawn into the page, so keep an unnumbered original.
Can I add a prefix or Bates numbers?
This tool prints plain numbers. Use Bates Numbering for prefixes and zero padding.
Is my PDF uploaded?
No. The numbers are added in your browser and the file never leaves your device.
