How to convert WebP to PDF

  1. Add your WebP images

    One or many. They stay on your device.

  2. Choose the quality

    Medium suits most photos. High keeps full pixels, Low gives the smallest file.

  3. Convert and save

    Press Convert to PDF. All images are saved in one PDF named from_webps.pdf.

What you get

Quality options
Option How images are stored Best for
High Full pixels, lightest compression Graphics, small text
Medium (default) JPEG about 75%, longest side up to 2500 px Photos
Low JPEG about 50%, longest side up to 1500 px Smallest file

WebP is the image format many websites save pictures in, and many programs cannot print or open it. Converting WebP to PDF puts the images on pages you can print, email or merge. This page does it in your browser, so your pictures are never uploaded.

One page per image

Every image goes on a separate page sized to the image, in the order of the list, and the whole set downloads as one file named from_webps.pdf. You can remove or reorder images in the list before converting.

Page size

The page matches the image's pixel size at one point per pixel, so a 1600 by 1200 image becomes a large 22 by 17 inch page. For standard A4 or Letter, run the result through Fix Page Size.

Quality, transparency and animation

Medium, the default, re-encodes as JPEG at about 75 percent and scales the longest side down to 2500 pixels; Low uses about 50 percent and 1500 pixels. Images are embedded as JPEG, which has no transparency, so see-through areas become a solid background. An animated WebP gives only its first frame.

Mixing formats

This page accepts .webp only. To combine WebP with JPG, PNG and other formats in one PDF, use Images to PDF.

How it works

Your browser decodes each WebP and a PDF library on your device writes the images into a new PDF. The pictures never leave your device.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Images saved from websites, screenshots and graphics exported as WebP that you need as a document: handouts, attachments, print sheets, portfolios.

  • Flatten transparent images onto a white background first if the background matters.
  • Reorder pages by moving the files in the list.

Questions and answers

How do I convert WebP to PDF?
Add the WebP images, choose the quality, press Convert to PDF and save.
Is WebP to PDF free?
Yes, with no account, no watermark and no limit on images.
Does each image become its own page?
Yes, in the order of the list, in one PDF.
What page size will the images get?
The image's pixel size at one point per pixel. Fix Page Size gives A4 or Letter.
What do the quality settings change?
High keeps full pixels; Medium and Low re-encode as JPEG at about 75% and 50% and limit the size.
Will transparent areas stay transparent?
No. They become a solid background because the images are embedded as JPEG.
Can I convert an animated WebP?
Only the first frame is used.
Are my images uploaded?
No. They are converted in your browser and never leave your device.
