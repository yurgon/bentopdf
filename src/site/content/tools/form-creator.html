How to create a fillable PDF

  1. Open a PDF or start blank

    Use your existing form, or create a blank Letter, A4 or custom page.

  2. Add the fields

    Choose a field type and place it where people should type, tick or choose. Drag to move and resize.

  3. Set the details

    Give each field a name, a default value, and mark it required or read-only.

  4. Download the form

    The fillable PDF is saved to your device, ready to send.

What you get

Field types
Field For
Text Names, addresses, any typed answer, single or multi-line
Checkbox Yes or no, I agree
Radio buttons One choice out of several
Dropdown and list Choosing from a fixed set of options
Date Dates in a set format
Signature A place to sign
Image A photo or logo
Barcode A code that encodes a field value
Button Reset or print the form

A fillable PDF has boxes people can type into, tick and choose from, instead of lines they must fill by hand. Making one usually means paying for Acrobat Pro. Here you add the fields in your browser, for free, and your document is never uploaded.

Make an existing PDF fillable

Open the form you already have, perhaps made in Word and saved as PDF, and place a field over each blank line and box. Fields that the PDF already has are read in, so you can also edit an old form: rename fields, change options or remove the ones you no longer need.

Start from a blank page

No document yet? Create a blank page in Letter, A4, A5, Legal, Tabloid, A3 or a custom size, add pages as you go, and build the form from scratch. A grid helps line fields up.

Will the form work for everyone?

Yes. The result is a standard PDF form (AcroForm), the kind Adobe Reader, Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Apple Preview and phone PDF apps can fill in. People do not need Plyleaf or an account to fill it; they can use Fill PDF Form or any PDF reader.

How it works

Your PDF is drawn page by page in the browser. Each field you place is written into the document as a real form field with its name, position, type and settings, by a PDF library running on your device. Nothing is sent to a server, and the finished form is saved straight to your device.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Registration and sign-up sheets, job and rental applications, order forms, permission slips, checklists, intake and feedback forms, and contracts with blanks to fill in.

  • No calculations or scripts. Fields hold what people type; totals are not worked out automatically.
  • Fields are placed by you. Lines and boxes in the document are not turned into fields automatically.
  • Collecting answers happens by email or however you share the file; there is no online form service here.

Questions and answers

How do I make a PDF fillable?
Open the PDF on this page, add a field over each blank, set its name and download. The result can be filled in any PDF reader.
Can I create a fillable PDF for free?
Yes, with no account, no Acrobat and no watermark.
Can I turn an existing PDF into a fillable form?
Yes. Open it and place fields over the blanks. Fields already in the file are read in and can be edited.
What field types can I add?
Text, checkbox, radio buttons, dropdown, list, date, signature, image, barcode and button.
Will the form work in Adobe Reader?
Yes. It is a standard PDF form that Reader, browsers and phone PDF apps can fill in.
Is my file uploaded?
No. The form is built in your browser and saved straight to your device.
