How to convert TIFF to PDF

  1. Add your TIFF files

    One or many, multi-page TIFFs included. They stay on your device.

  2. Choose the quality

    High keeps every pixel. Medium and Low give a much smaller file.

  3. Convert and save

    Press Convert to PDF. All pages are saved in one PDF.

What you get

TIFF is the format of scanners, fax software and archives, and few apps open it easily. Converting TIFF to PDF gives you a file anyone can view, print and email. This page does it in your browser, so scans of contracts, medical records or ID documents are never uploaded.

Multi-page TIFF to one PDF

Every page inside a TIFF becomes its own PDF page, so a 10 page fax file gives a 10 page PDF without splitting it first. Pages from several TIFF files are appended in list order into one document.

Choose the quality

High stores each page as lossless PNG. Medium and Low re-encode pages as JPEG at 75% and 50%, which is much smaller for scanned photos but can blur fine text. Pixel dimensions stay the same in all three.

Which TIFF files work

Grayscale and color images at 8, 16 or 32 bits per channel, with or without transparency, stored uncompressed or with LZW or Deflate compression. TIFFs that use fax compression (CCITT Group 3 or 4), PackBits or JPEG cannot be read here; re-save them as uncompressed or LZW TIFF in an image editor first.

Page size and searching

Pages are sized by pixels, one point per pixel, and the TIFF's DPI tag is ignored. A 300 DPI Letter scan becomes a large page, so run the result through Fix Page Size for true Letter or A4. The PDF contains images only; to make it searchable, use PDF OCR.

How it works

Your browser decodes the TIFF pages and a PDF library on your device puts each page into a new PDF. 16 and 32 bit images are reduced to 8 bits per channel. Nothing is sent to a server.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Fax and scanner archives, patent and legal documents, architectural and engineering scans, mailroom and records digitizing.

  • Very large TIFFs take time, because every page is decoded in your browser.
  • Scientific TIFFs lose range when reduced to 8 bits per channel.

Questions and answers

How do I convert TIFF to PDF?
Add the TIFF files, choose the quality, press Convert to PDF and save.
Does it handle multi-page TIFF files?
Yes. Every page becomes a PDF page, and several files are joined in list order.
Is TIFF to PDF free?
Yes, with no account, no watermark and no limit on files.
Which TIFF types are supported?
Grayscale and color, 8 to 32 bits, uncompressed or LZW or Deflate. CCITT fax, PackBits and JPEG TIFFs are not supported.
What do the quality options do?
High keeps pixels lossless. Medium and Low use JPEG at 75% and 50% for a smaller file.
Why is the page size so large?
Pages use one point per pixel and ignore the DPI tag. Fix Page Size gives true Letter or A4.
Will the PDF be searchable?
No, it contains images. Run PDF OCR to add searchable text.
Are my scans uploaded?
No. The conversion runs in your browser and the files never leave your device.
