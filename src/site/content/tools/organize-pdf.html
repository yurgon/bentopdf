How to rearrange PDF pages

  1. Add your PDF

    One file. Every page appears as a thumbnail on your device.

  2. Drag pages into place

    Drag a thumbnail to its new spot, or type the new order, such as 3, 1, 2, 4, and press Apply Order. Duplicate or delete pages with the buttons on each thumbnail.

  3. Save the new order

    Press Save Changes. The rearranged PDF downloads under the original name.

To rearrange PDF pages is to change the order of the pages in a file: move a page earlier, swap two pages, or put a scanned stack back in sequence. Here you do it on thumbnails in your browser and save a new PDF, so the document is never uploaded.

Drag pages into a new order

Every page shows as a thumbnail. Drag one to its new position and the page number labels update as you go. It is the quickest way to fix one page that landed in the wrong place.

Type a new page order

For a whole-document change, type the order in the Page Order box, for example 3, 1, 2, 4, and press Apply Order. The list must name every original page exactly once: a missing page or a repeat is rejected. To copy or remove pages, use the duplicate and delete buttons.

Duplicate and delete pages

Each click on the green button adds another copy of a page right after it, and every copy can be dragged anywhere. That is handy for several blank copies of a form page. The red button drops a page from the result. To remove many pages by range, Delete Pages is quicker.

Quality, bookmarks and other files

Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and images keep their resolution. Bookmarks are not carried over, because the new file is built from copied pages; add them again with the Bookmark tool. This tool works on one file; to take pages from several PDFs, first merge them.

How it works

The thumbnails are drawn by your browser. When you press Save Changes, a PDF library on your device builds a new file from copies of the pages in your order and saves it under the original name. The document never leaves your device.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Scans fed in the wrong order, chapters to move, appendices to put first, forms to duplicate, slide handouts to resequence.

  • Keep the original. The result replaces the page order for good, and the outline is lost.
  • Sideways pages? Fix them with Rotate PDF.

Questions and answers

How do I rearrange pages in a PDF?
Add the file, drag the thumbnails into the new order and press Save Changes.
Can I type the new page order?
Yes. Enter the original page numbers in the new order, such as 3, 1, 2, 4, and press Apply Order. Every page must appear exactly once.
How do I move one page to another position?
Drag its thumbnail to the new spot. The page numbers update as you go.
Can I duplicate a page?
Yes. Each click on the green button adds another copy right after the page.
Can I delete pages here too?
Yes, with the red button on a thumbnail. The last remaining page cannot be deleted.
Does reordering keep my bookmarks?
No. Page content, images and annotations are kept, but bookmarks are not. Add them again with the Bookmark tool.
Does it reduce quality?
No. Pages are copied as they are, so text stays selectable and images keep their resolution.
Is my PDF uploaded?
No. The pages are rearranged in your browser and the file never leaves your device.
