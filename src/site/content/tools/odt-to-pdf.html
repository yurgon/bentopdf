How to convert ODT to PDF

  1. Add your ODT files

    One or many. They stay on your device.

  2. Press Convert to PDF

    The first conversion downloads the converter, about 77 MB, once.

  3. Save the PDFs

    One file downloads as a PDF; several come as a ZIP.

ODT is the OpenDocument text format used by LibreOffice, OpenOffice and many other programs. Converting ODT to PDF gives you a fixed document anyone can open and print, without LibreOffice. It runs in your browser, so the document is never uploaded.

Convert without LibreOffice

Drop the .odt file on the page and press Convert to PDF. A build of LibreOffice compiled for the browser does the conversion, so you need no LibreOffice, OpenOffice or Word on the computer.

Formatting, tables and images

The conversion uses LibreOffice's own PDF export, so styles, tables, images and page layout come through as Writer would print them. The text is real text, so it can be selected, copied and searched. Documents with embedded OLE objects or fonts that are not available may differ slightly, so check one sample before a big batch.

Why the first file is slower

The engine is large and is downloaded and started the first time you convert, with a progress indicator. After that it starts at once, even offline. Any standard OpenDocument text file works, whichever program saved it.

Several files and other formats

Add as many .odt files as you like. One downloads as a PDF with the original name; two or more arrive in odt-converted.zip. Word files go through Word to PDF, which uses the same engine.

How it works

A LibreOffice engine compiled for the browser opens the ODT and exports it to PDF on your device. It is downloaded once, about 77 MB, and runs locally. Your documents are never sent to a server.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Letters, reports, theses and notes written in LibreOffice or OpenOffice that you need to send, print or archive as PDF.

  • Only .odt is accepted. Open and save an .ott template as .odt first.
  • Combine the results with Merge PDF.

Questions and answers

How do I convert ODT to PDF without LibreOffice?
Add the .odt file and press Convert to PDF. The converter runs inside your browser.
Is ODT to PDF free?
Yes, with no account, no watermark and no limit on files.
Will formatting, tables and images be preserved?
Yes. LibreOffice's own PDF export keeps styles, tables, images and layout.
Why is the first conversion slower?
The converter, about 77 MB, is downloaded once. After that it starts at once.
Can I convert several ODT files at once?
Yes. One file gives a PDF; several are saved together as a ZIP.
What about DOCX, DOC or OTT files?
Only .odt works here. Use Word to PDF for Word files, and save .ott as .odt first.
Will the PDF text be searchable?
Yes. The PDF is exported from the real text, not a picture.
Is my document uploaded?
No. It is converted in your browser and never leaves your device.
