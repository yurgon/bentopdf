How to make a PDF booklet

  1. Add your PDF

    Drop the file onto the page or choose it. Pages are counted right here in your browser, so even a private document is safe to use.

  2. Pick the booklet size

    A4 paper folds into A5, US Letter into half Letter, A3 into A4. Choose right binding for Arabic, Hebrew or manga.

  3. Check the preview

    Each sheet shows which pages land on its front and back. If the page count is not a multiple of four, blank pages are added at the end.

  4. Print, fold, staple

    Follow the printing guide for your printer, fold the whole stack in half and put two staples on the crease.

A PDF booklet is your document rearranged so that, printed on both sides and folded in half, it reads like a small book. You do not need special software: this page does the rearranging and tells you exactly how to feed the paper.

How to print a booklet on a home printer

Most printing trouble comes from one setting: which edge the printer flips the paper on. For a booklet it has to flip on the short edge. If it flips on the long edge, every back side comes out upside down.

If your printer prints both sides

  1. Open the booklet PDF and choose Print.
  2. Set the paper to the size you picked (A4 for an A5 booklet, Letter for half Letter).
  3. Turn on two-sided printing and choose flip on short edge.
  4. Set scale to 100% or “Actual size”, not “Fit to page”.

If it prints one side at a time

Choose “One side at a time” above. The booklet comes out as two passes. Print pass 1, put the printed stack back into the tray without changing its order, turned the way the guide shows for your printer, then print pass 2. Not sure which way your printer feeds paper? Print a two-page test first: mark the top of the first sheet with a pencil before pass 2 and you will see how the printer turns it.

How it works

Printers call this imposition. A 16-page booklet is four sheets nested inside each other, so the outer sheet carries pages 16 and 1 on the front and 2 and 15 on the back. The tool works out that order for any page count, places two pages side by side on each sheet and scales them to fit. Pages are moved, not re-rendered, so text and vector graphics stay sharp.

Everything happens in your browser. The PDF is opened in memory, rearranged and saved back to your device. Nothing is sent to a server, which is why there is no file size limit other than your device’s memory.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Zines and comics, event programs, recipe booklets, children’s stories, instruction manuals, church and wedding programs, short story collections for an e-reader-free afternoon.

  • Long documents. More than about 40 pages (10 sheets) will not fold flat and will be hard to staple. Split the PDF into parts of 32 or 40 pages and make a booklet from each.
  • Creep. In a thick booklet the inner pages stick out slightly after folding. This tool does not shift pages to compensate, so keep important content away from the outer edge.
  • Landscape pages are rotated and scaled down to fit the half sheet, so small text gets smaller.
  • Password-protected PDFs need to be unlocked first.

Which paper size to choose

Pick the paper you have in the printer. Your pages are scaled to the booklet size: an A4 document printed as an A5 booklet is reduced to 71%, about the same as reading a paperback. If the text becomes too small, a booklet of A4 pages on A3 paper keeps it at full size.

Questions and answers

How do I print a PDF as a booklet?
Make the booklet here, then print it two-sided with “flip on short edge” and the scale at 100%. Fold the stack in half and staple along the fold. If your printer cannot print both sides, choose “One side at a time” and print the two passes.
Why are my booklet pages in the wrong order?
Usually the printer flipped on the long edge, so every back side is upside down or shifted. Reprint with “flip on short edge”. If the order is wrong even with that, check that the print dialog is not set to print a page range or to reverse order.
Should I flip on the short edge or the long edge?
Short edge. A booklet sheet is landscape, and its back side must turn over like a calendar page. Long edge flipping is for ordinary portrait documents.
How many pages does a booklet need?
A multiple of four, because each sheet holds four pages. If your PDF has 14 pages, the tool adds two blank pages at the end to make 16. You can see where they go in the preview before printing.
Can I make a booklet without a duplex printer?
Yes. Choose “One side at a time”. You get the fronts in one pass and the backs in a second pass, with instructions on how to put the stack back in the tray.
What paper size should I use?
The paper you already have. A4 makes an A5 booklet, US Letter makes a half-Letter booklet (5.5 × 8.5 in), and A3 makes an A4 booklet with pages at full size.
Is my file uploaded?
No. The PDF is read and rearranged in your browser and saved straight to your device. You can disconnect from the internet after the page loads and the tool still works.
Can I make a right-to-left booklet?
Yes. Choose right binding. The page order is mirrored so the booklet opens from the right, as Arabic, Hebrew and Japanese books do.
