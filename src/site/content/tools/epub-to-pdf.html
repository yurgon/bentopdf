How to convert an EPUB to PDF

  1. Add the EPUB

    Choose one ebook or several. Only DRM-free EPUB files can be opened.

  2. Let it convert

    The first time, the converter loads into your browser. A long novel takes a few seconds more.

  3. Save the PDF

    One book gives one PDF; several come as a ZIP. Open it in any PDF reader or print it.

What you get

EPUB is the standard ebook format: the text flows to fit whatever screen it is read on, which is great on an e-reader and awkward everywhere else. A PDF has fixed pages that print, share and open in any viewer. This converter turns EPUB to PDF in your browser, so your library is not uploaded to anyone.

How the pages are laid out

An EPUB has no pages, only chapters. The converter pours the text into pages, starting each chapter where the book says it should and keeping images, headings, italics and bold. The book's own style sheet decides fonts and spacing, so a well-made ebook gives a pleasant PDF and a carelessly made one may have odd spacing.

Protected ebooks

Books bought from Kindle, Apple Books, Kobo and most other stores are usually locked with DRM so that they open only in that store's app. Such files cannot be converted here or by any honest tool. DRM-free books work: from Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, many independent publishers and authors, or your own writing exported as EPUB. For DRM-free Kindle files there is MOBI to PDF.

Printing an ebook

To print a short book or a story as a booklet you can fold and staple, convert it here, then open the PDF in the booklet maker. It arranges the pages for double-sided printing and tells you how to feed the paper.

How it works

An EPUB is a ZIP of web pages, images and a style sheet. The converter opens it in your browser, lays out the chapters into pages with a document engine, and writes a PDF. The engine is downloaded the first time and then kept, so later conversions start faster. The book never leaves your device.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Printing a public-domain classic, reading on a laptop or a tablet without an ebook app, sharing a self-published book with a reviewer, keeping course readings as one PDF to annotate.

  • DRM-protected books cannot be opened.
  • Page numbers will not match a printed edition of the same book; the pages are made by the converter.
  • Interactive content such as audio, video and scripts is dropped; a PDF page cannot play it.

Questions and answers

How do I convert EPUB to PDF?
Add the EPUB on this page and press Convert to PDF. The PDF is saved to your device; several books come in a ZIP.
Is it free to convert EPUB to PDF?
Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no limit on the number of books.
Are images and formatting kept?
Yes. Images, headings, italics, bold and chapter breaks come through, styled by the book’s own style sheet.
Can I convert Kindle or Apple Books purchases?
Not if they are protected with DRM, which store purchases usually are. DRM-free EPUB and MOBI files convert fine.
Is my ebook uploaded?
No. The book is opened and converted in your browser and the PDF is saved directly to your device.
Can I convert several EPUB files at once?
Yes. Add them all and you get a ZIP with one PDF per book.
How do I print an EPUB as a book?
Convert it to PDF here, then open the PDF in the booklet maker, which arranges the pages for folding and stapling.
Why do the page numbers differ from the printed book?
An EPUB has no pages of its own, so the converter makes them. Page size and font decide where each page ends, which never matches a printed edition exactly.
