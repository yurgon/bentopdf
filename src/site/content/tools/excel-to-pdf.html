How to convert Excel to PDF

  1. Add your spreadsheets

    XLSX, XLS, ODS or CSV, one or many. They stay on your device.

  2. Set the layout in Excel first

    The converter reads orientation, print area and scaling from the file, so set them in Excel or Calc before you add it.

  3. Convert and save

    Press Convert to PDF. One file downloads as a PDF; several come as a ZIP.

Converting Excel to PDF gives you a fixed copy of a spreadsheet that looks the same on every screen and cannot be changed by accident. It is the usual way to send a budget, invoice or report. Here the conversion runs in your browser, so financial data and personal records are never uploaded.

XLSX, XLS, ODS and CSV

Add Excel workbooks (.xlsx and .xls), OpenDocument spreadsheets from LibreOffice Calc (.ods) and .csv files. You can mix formats in one batch. For a plain CSV table without formatting, CSV to PDF is lighter and faster.

Stop wide tables being cut off

The tool has no layout options of its own. It follows each sheet's page setup, print area and scaling, so the Print Preview in Excel is a good guide to the result. Switch to landscape, set the print area and choose Fit all columns on one page in Excel or Calc before you add the file.

All sheets, charts and formatting

Every sheet goes into the PDF. Charts are drawn into the pages, and fills, borders, fonts and number formats are kept. Formulas appear as their calculated values, not as formula text.

Several files at once

One spreadsheet downloads as a PDF with the same name. Two or more are packed into a ZIP with one PDF each, and duplicate names are numbered.

How it works

A LibreOffice engine compiled for the browser opens the workbook and saves it as a PDF on your device. The first conversion downloads the engine, about 77 MB, once; after that it starts at once, even offline. Your spreadsheets are never sent to a server.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Invoices, quotes, budgets, timetables, price lists, financial reports and class registers that must look the same for everyone.

  • Fonts that are not available are replaced by the closest substitute, so line breaks can shift slightly.
  • Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Indic text needs fonts that the browser engine does not include, so check those files.
  • Combine the results with Merge PDF.

Questions and answers

How do I convert Excel to PDF?
Add the XLSX, XLS, ODS or CSV file, press Convert to PDF and save the result.
Does it convert all sheets or only the first?
All sheets are included, each following its own page setup, print area and scaling.
How do I stop columns being cut off?
Set landscape and fit-to-width scaling in Excel before you add the file. The converter uses those settings.
Will formulas and charts survive?
Charts are drawn into the PDF and formulas appear as their values. Cell formatting is kept.
Is Excel to PDF free?
Yes, with no account, no watermark and no limit on files.
Can I convert several workbooks at once?
Yes. One file downloads as a PDF; several are saved together as a ZIP with one PDF each.
Why does the first conversion take a while?
The converter engine, about 77 MB, is downloaded once. After that conversions start at once.
Is my spreadsheet uploaded?
No. The workbook is converted in your browser and never leaves your device.
