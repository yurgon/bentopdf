How to flatten a PDF

  1. Add your PDFs

    One file or several. They stay on your device.

  2. Check what will be flattened

    Form fields, annotations and other interactive elements become fixed page content.

  3. Flatten and save

    Press Flatten PDF(s). One file downloads directly; several come as a ZIP.

What you get

What flattening changes
Element After flattening Note
Form fields Fixed text and ticks on the page Cannot be filled again
Comments, highlights, stamps Burned into the page Pop-up notes are lost
Text, images, bookmarks Unchanged Text stays selectable
Links May stop working Links are annotations too

Flattening a PDF merges its interactive layers into the page itself. A filled form, comments and highlights look exactly the same afterward, but nobody can move, edit or delete them. It is the usual last step before you send a filled form or share a marked-up document.

Lock a filled form

After you fill a form, flatten it and the answers become part of the page, so recipients cannot change them. Do it only when you are done, because there is no way to unflatten a PDF. Fill the form first with Fill PDF Form.

What gets flattened

Form fields (text boxes, checkboxes, radio buttons, dropdowns, signature fields), comments, highlights, strikethroughs, sticky notes, stamps and ink marks. The page looks the same, but it is now fixed content. If the file has annotations but no form, you simply see a message that no form was found; the annotations are still flattened.

Text stays selectable

Flattening does not turn pages into images. Regular text, images, bookmarks and page sizes are untouched. If you want every page turned into a picture, use Rasterize PDF instead.

Links and comments

Comments and highlights stay visible but lose their pop-up notes and can no longer be edited. Links are annotations as well, so a clickable link may stop working even though its text remains on the page. Flatten before you sign, not after, so the signed content cannot change.

How it works

A PDF library on your device draws each form field and annotation into the page content and removes the interactive objects, then saves a new file. Nothing is uploaded.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Completed applications and tax forms, contracts with filled blanks, reviewed documents you want to share without the review tools, forms that look empty in some viewers.

  • Keep an editable copy. A flattened PDF cannot be filled or edited again.
  • Several files at once come back as flattened_pdfs.zip; a single file is saved with flattened_ added to its name.

Questions and answers

What does it mean to flatten a PDF?
It merges form fields, comments and other interactive elements into the page, so they look the same but cannot be edited.
How do I flatten a PDF so it cannot be edited?
Add the file, press Flatten PDF(s) and save the result.
Will the text still be selectable after flattening?
Yes. Pages are not turned into images; only form fields and annotations become fixed content.
I filled out a form. Should I flatten it?
Yes, once you are finished, to lock the answers. There is no way to unflatten a PDF.
Do links and comments survive flattening?
Comments and highlights stay visible but lose their notes. Links may stop working.
Can I flatten several PDFs at once?
Yes. Several files are saved together as a ZIP with one flattened PDF each.
Should I flatten before or after signing?
Before, so form values and marks cannot change after the signature.
Is it private?
Yes. Flattening runs in your browser and the file is never uploaded.
