  1. Add both files

    Put the original on the left and the new version on the right.

  2. Look through the changes

    Differences are highlighted and listed. Click one in the list to jump to it in both files.

  3. Save a report if you need one

    Export the comparison as a PDF, in overlay, split or alternating layout, to send to someone else.

Comparing PDFs answers one question: what is different in the new version? Reading two contracts line by line takes an hour and still misses things. This tool finds every change in seconds, shows it in place and lists it, and runs in your browser, so confidential documents are never uploaded.

Compare two PDFs side by side or as an overlay

Side by side shows both files with scrolling kept in step, which is the easiest way to read a changed paragraph. Overlay puts one page over the other, useful for plans, drawings and forms, where a small shift matters more than the words. Flicker swaps the two versions in place so moved items catch your eye. You can zoom, fit the page, and switch between all changes and content only.

Find text, image and formatting changes

Each difference is sorted as added, deleted, modified or moved, and by what changed: text, images, headers, annotations, formatting or backgrounds. Counts are shown for each kind, and you can filter the list to see, for example, only deleted text. A paragraph that was moved is reported as moved, not as one deletion plus one addition.

Compare scanned PDFs with OCR

A scan is a picture of a page with no text inside. Turn on OCR and the text is read from the images first, then compared. Recognition takes longer and is only as good as the scan, so check important changes by eye. To make a scan searchable for good, use PDF OCR.

How it works

Both files are read in your browser. Text is extracted with its position on the page, matched between the two versions, and the differences are worked out and drawn on top of the pages. When page numbers shift because something was added near the start, the text is still matched correctly. The report is built in the browser too. Nothing is sent anywhere.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Contracts and leases from the other side, revised drafts, legal redlines, proofs from a printer, updated price lists, insurance policies at renewal, and school or government forms that changed this year.

  • Two PDFs only. To compare a Word file, save it as PDF first, or use Word to PDF.
  • Scans depend on OCR quality: blurred or skewed pages produce false changes.
  • Password-protected PDFs need the password, which the tool asks for.

Questions and answers

How can I compare two PDF files for differences?
Add the original and the new version on this page. Every difference is highlighted in both files and listed with its type, and you can jump from the list to each one.
Can I compare scanned PDFs?
Yes. Turn on OCR and the text is recognized from the scan first. Clear, straight scans give the best result.
Is this the same as a redline?
It does the same job: shows what was added, deleted and changed. Export the comparison as a PDF to share it like a redline.
Can I track changes in a PDF?
A PDF does not record its edits the way Word does. Comparing the old and new versions here gives you the same list of changes.
Does it detect moved paragraphs?
Yes. Moved text is reported as moved, and it is still matched when page numbers shift.
Can I compare a PDF with a Word document?
Convert the Word file to PDF first with Word to PDF, then compare the two PDFs.
Are my files uploaded?
No. Both files are compared in your browser, which makes it safe for contracts and other confidential documents.
Is it free?
Yes, with no account, no page limit and no watermark on the report.
