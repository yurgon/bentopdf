How to convert RTF to PDF

  1. Add your RTF files

    One file or several. They stay on your device.

  2. Press Convert to PDF

    The first conversion downloads the converter, about 77 MB, once.

  3. Save the PDFs

    One file downloads as a PDF; several come as a ZIP, one PDF each.

RTF, Rich Text Format, is an old document format written by WordPad, TextEdit and many records systems. Converting RTF to PDF gives you a fixed copy that looks the same for everyone and is easy to print and email. The conversion runs in your browser, so letters, notes and medical or legal records are never uploaded.

What formatting is kept

Bold and italic, paragraph alignment, lists, tables and embedded images come through as they would if you exported the file from LibreOffice Writer. Embedded objects, such as an inserted spreadsheet, may not render fully.

Fonts and languages

Only fonts bundled with the engine are available. They cover common Latin, Arabic and Hebrew faces; other fonts are replaced by the closest substitute, so line breaks and spacing can shift a little. Chinese, Japanese, Korean and Indic text may appear with a fallback font or as boxes.

Several files at once

One file downloads as a PDF with the same base name. Several files come together as a ZIP with one PDF each, and duplicate names are numbered.

RTF to PDF or Word to PDF

Both use the same engine and give the same result for RTF. Word to PDF also accepts DOC, DOCX and ODT, so use it when your batch mixes formats.

How it works

A LibreOffice engine compiled for the browser opens the RTF and saves it as a PDF on your device. The engine is about 77 MB, downloaded once on the first conversion; after that it starts at once, even offline. Your documents are never sent to a server.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Letters and CVs saved from WordPad or TextEdit, exports from legal and medical software, old documents kept in RTF.

  • Proprietary extensions in some RTF files may be dropped; the text and standard formatting still convert.
  • Plain text files are simpler with TXT to PDF.

Questions and answers

How do I convert RTF to PDF?
Add the RTF file, press Convert to PDF and save the result.
Is RTF to PDF free?
Yes, with no account, no watermark and no limit on files.
Is the formatting preserved?
Bold, italic, alignment, lists, tables and images are kept. Embedded objects may not render fully.
Why does the first conversion take longer?
The converter engine, about 77 MB, is downloaded once. After that conversions are quick.
Can I convert several RTF files at once?
Yes. One file saves as a PDF; several are saved together as a ZIP.
Will my fonts look the same?
Common Latin, Arabic and Hebrew fonts are available; others are replaced by the closest one, so spacing can shift.
Does it work with Chinese, Japanese or Korean text?
Those fonts are not bundled, so such text may show with a fallback font or as boxes.
Is my document uploaded?
No. It is converted in your browser and never leaves your device.
