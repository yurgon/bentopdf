How to split a PDF

  1. Add your PDF

    Choose one file. It is opened on your device, not uploaded.

  2. Choose how to split

    Type a range such as 1-3, 7, save every page separately, or pick another of the six modes.

  3. Split and save

    One part is saved as a PDF; several parts come together in a ZIP.

To split a PDF is to cut one document into smaller ones: a few pages for one person, each chapter as its own file, or every page separately. This splitter runs in your browser, so your file is not uploaded, and there is no limit on pages or parts.

Extract pages from a PDF

Choose page range and list the pages you want, separated by commas, with a hyphen for ranges: 1-3, 7, 12-15. You can save them as one combined PDF or one PDF per range. The original stays as it was; you only get a new file with the pages you chose.

Split every page into its own file

Every page becomes a separate PDF, named in order and packed into one ZIP. This is handy when a scanner put a stack of receipts, invoices or forms into one long file. To keep only odd or even pages, for example from a scanner that scanned front sides first, use even or odd pages.

Split by bookmarks or into equal parts

If the PDF has bookmarks, it can be cut where they start, so each chapter or section becomes its own file. Choose the bookmark level: top level for chapters, deeper levels for sections. Without bookmarks, split every N pages, for example into parts of 10 pages for a portal that only takes short files. Or click page thumbnails to pick pages by eye.

How it works

The PDF is read in your browser and the chosen pages are copied into new documents with a PDF library running on your device. Text, images and web links on the copied pages stay as they were, and nothing is recompressed, so quality does not change. The bookmark list is not copied into the parts. Several parts are packed into a ZIP in memory and saved to your device.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Sending one chapter of a book, one statement out of a year, the signed page of a contract, or a long scan in parts that fit an upload limit.

  • Splitting does not make pages smaller. If a part is still too large, use Compress PDF.
  • Bookmarks mode needs bookmarks. Many scans have none; use page ranges or every N pages instead.
  • Password-protected PDFs need the password, which the tool asks for.
  • To cut one page in half, for example a scan with two book pages side by side, splitting is not the right tool; it works with whole pages.

Questions and answers

How do I split a PDF into separate pages?
Add the file, choose split all pages into separate files and press Split. Every page is saved as its own PDF inside a ZIP.
How do I extract pages from a PDF?
Choose page range and type the pages, such as 2-5, 9. You get a new PDF with just those pages.
Can I split a PDF without Adobe?
Yes. This page does it in your browser on any computer or phone, with no software to install.
Is splitting a PDF free?
Yes, with no account, no watermark and no limit on pages or files.
Can I split a PDF by chapter?
Yes, if it has bookmarks. Choose split by bookmarks and the level to cut at.
Will splitting reduce quality?
No. Pages are copied as they are, without recompression.
Are my files uploaded?
No. The PDF is split in your browser and the parts are saved straight to your device.
