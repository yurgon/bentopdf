How to convert a PDF to Word

  1. Add the PDF

    Choose one file or several. Locked files ask for their password.

  2. Wait for the conversion

    The first time, the converter loads into your browser, which takes a few seconds. Later runs start at once.

  3. Open it in Word

    Save the DOCX and open it in Word, Pages, Google Docs or LibreOffice. Several files come as a ZIP.

What you get

A PDF is made for reading and printing; Word is made for writing. Converting PDF to Word rebuilds the page as a document again: lines become paragraphs, grids become tables, and you can edit, restyle and reuse the text. This converter does it on your device, so confidential reports and contracts are not handed to a server.

What keeps its formatting, and what may shift

Ordinary documents convert best: letters, reports, CVs, articles and simple forms. Paragraphs, headings, bold and italic, bulleted lists, images and most tables come through. Layouts built for print are harder. Text in several columns, captions placed over pictures and tables without lines may need tidying in Word afterwards. Fonts that are not on your computer are replaced by similar ones, so line breaks can move.

Scanned PDFs: run OCR first

A scan or a photo saved as PDF contains a picture of text, not text. Converting it to Word gives you that picture inside a Word page, which is no more editable than before. Run the file through OCR PDF to recognise the words, then convert the result here.

When you do not need Word at all

If you only want to fix a typo, change a date or delete a line, converting and converting back risks the layout for nothing. Change the text in the PDF itself with the PDF editor. Need a PDF again after editing in Word? Use Word to PDF.

Can Word open a PDF by itself?

Desktop Word for Windows, 2013 and later, opens a PDF through File, Open and converts it with its own engine. It needs an Office licence, and complicated pages come out much as they do here. Word on the web and Google Docs keep only the text of most PDFs. This page needs no licence and handles several files at once.

How it works

The converter reads each page, works out lines, paragraphs, columns and table cells from the positions of the letters and lines, and writes a DOCX file with the same structure. Images are copied in, and colours set for print are converted so Word shows them correctly. All of it runs in your browser with a conversion engine that is downloaded once and then kept; your PDF is never sent anywhere.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Updating an old CV that only exists as a PDF, reusing a report or a contract as a template, quoting long passages, translating a document, fixing a form someone sent you.

  • Password-protected PDFs need the password. Files that forbid copying are converted only if you can open them.
  • Very long or heavy PDFs of several hundred pages take a while, and a phone may run out of memory. Split them with Split PDF first.
  • Exact print layouts such as brochures and magazines will not match one to one; Word is not a page layout program.

Questions and answers

How can I convert a PDF to Word for free?
Add the PDF on this page and press Convert to DOCX. The Word file is saved to your device. It is free, with no sign up and no limit on files.
Will the formatting stay the same?
For ordinary documents, mostly yes: paragraphs, headings, lists, images and tables carry over. Multi-column print layouts and text on top of pictures may need tidying in Word.
Can I convert a scanned PDF to Word?
Run it through OCR PDF first. A scan holds a picture of text, and without recognition the Word file would contain only that picture.
Is my file uploaded?
No. The conversion runs in your browser and the DOCX is saved directly to your device. Nothing is sent to a server.
Can I convert several PDFs at once?
Yes. Add as many as you like; you get a ZIP with one Word file for each PDF.
Does it work with password-protected PDFs?
Yes, if you know the password. The tool asks for it before converting.
How do I convert a PDF form to Word?
Convert it like any other PDF: the layout, labels and lines come through. Fillable fields are not turned into Word form fields, so if you only need to fill the form in, use Fill PDF forms instead.
Why does the first conversion take longer?
The conversion engine is downloaded into your browser the first time. After that it is stored and later conversions start straight away, even offline.
