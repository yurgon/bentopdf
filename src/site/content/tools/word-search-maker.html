How to make a word search

  1. Type your words

    One word or short phrase per line, or paste a list separated by commas.

  2. Choose size and difficulty

    Pick a grid or leave it on Auto. Easy, Medium or Hard sets which ways words can run.

  3. Make versions for the class

    Same words, a different grid in each version, so neighbors cannot copy.

  4. Print puzzles and keys

    Print the puzzles and the answer keys, or save everything as one PDF.

What you get

This word search maker hides your own words in a printable letter grid and makes as many different versions as your class needs, each with its answer key. It is free, needs no account and works in your browser, so your word list is never uploaded.

Create your own word search

Type one word per line, or paste a list separated by commas: spelling words, science vocabulary, names of the children in a class, things that belong to a holiday. Phrases work too; the spaces are left out of the grid and the list under the puzzle shows them as typed. Repeated words are counted once. Letters that fill the empty squares are taken from your own words, so the grid never gives a word away by an odd-looking letter.

Auto picks a grid that holds the longest word with room for the rest. Choose 10×10 for young children and 15×15 or 20×20 for long lists or a tougher search. If a word is longer than the grid or the list does not fit, the tool names those words so you can choose a bigger grid.

Different versions for a class

Set Different versions to the number of students, or to two or three for alternate rows. Every version uses the same words in a new grid, so a neighbor’s sheet does not help. Versions are numbered at the top of each page, and New puzzles shuffles the whole set again.

Answer keys

Each answer key shows the grid with every word marked by a highlighter stripe. A single puzzle gets a full page key; a class set gets four keys per page, labeled with the version number, so checking thirty sheets takes a few pages, not thirty.

How it works

The word search generator runs in your browser. It places the longest words first, in random positions and in the directions the level allows, lets words share a letter where they cross, and tries again until every word fits. Each version gets its own random seed. Print opens your browser’s print dialog at real size; Download PDF saves puzzles and keys in one file on A4 or Letter.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Spelling and vocabulary practice, early finisher work, substitute teacher plans, holiday and party activities, activity sheets for care homes and long trips.

  • Playing online is not part of this tool: the puzzles are for printing.
  • Pictures in place of the word list are not supported yet.
  • Chinese, Japanese, Korean and emoji characters are not in the print font. Latin, Cyrillic and Greek letters work.

Questions and answers

Is this word search maker free?
Yes. Make and print as many puzzles as you need, with answer keys, no account and no watermark.
How many words fit in a word search?
A 15×15 grid holds about 15 to 20 words of average length. Auto picks a size for your list, and the tool tells you if a word did not fit.
What does each difficulty level do?
Easy runs words across and down. Medium adds diagonals. Hard adds every backward direction, so words can read right to left or bottom to top.
Can every student get a different puzzle?
Yes. Set Different versions to the class size. Each version has the same words in a different grid and is numbered at the top.
Is there an answer key?
Yes. Every version has a key with the words highlighted: one full page for a single puzzle, four keys per page for a class set.
Can I hide the word list?
Yes. Turn off Words to find for a harder puzzle where students know the topic but not the words.
Can I use words with spaces or other alphabets?
Spaces and hyphens are left out of the grid. Accented Latin letters, Cyrillic and Greek work, and the filler letters come from your own words.
Is my word list uploaded?
No. The puzzles are made on your device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.
