How to merge PDF files

  1. Add your PDFs

    Drop all the files at once or one by one. JPG and PNG images can go in the same stack and become pages.

  2. Put them in order

    Drag files into the order you want. Switch to page mode to move single pages between files.

  3. Pick pages if needed

    Leave the pages box empty to keep a whole file, or type a range such as 1-3, 5 to keep only those.

  4. Merge and save

    Press Merge PDFs. The combined file is saved to your device, usually in Downloads.

What you get

To merge PDF files is to put several documents into one, page after page, so you send, print or archive a single file. This page does it in your browser: no software to install, no account, and the documents are never sent anywhere.

Whole files or single pages

File mode is the quick way. Each file is one card in a list; drag the cards into order and every page of every file goes into the result. Use it to join chapters, put a cover letter in front of a CV, or collect a month of invoices.

Page mode shows a thumbnail of every page from every file. Drag single pages anywhere: put page 3 of one document between two pages of another, or move a signed page back into a contract. It is slower to set up but gives exact control.

Merge only some pages

In file mode each file has a pages box. Leave it empty to take everything. Type 1-3, 5 to take pages one to three and five, or 2 for a single page. Ranges are counted in that file, so you do not need to work out where they land in the final document. Need the opposite, one file broken into parts? Use Split PDF.

Merge PDF and JPG in one file

Photos of receipts, a scanned ID or a screenshot can join the same stack. Each image becomes a page the size of the picture, placed where you drag it. For a stack of photos only, with no PDFs, JPG to PDF is quicker.

How it works

The tool reads each PDF in your browser, copies the pages you chose into a new document in the order you set, and saves it. Pages are copied, not printed again or converted to images, so text stays sharp and searchable and links inside pages keep working. Original page numbering is reset to 1, 2, 3 unless you keep each file's own page labels.

Nothing is uploaded, which is why there is no limit on the number of files or their size other than your device's memory. Once the page has loaded you can even disconnect from the internet.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Job applications (letter, CV and certificates in one file), tax and visa paperwork, contracts with signed pages, school assignments, scanned pages from a phone, monthly reports.

  • Password-protected PDFs ask for the password first. Without it a locked file cannot be opened, here or anywhere else.
  • Fillable forms from different files can clash if their fields share names. Fill them in first, or flatten them with Flatten PDF.
  • Digital signatures do not survive merging: the merged file is a new document, so signature checks will fail. Keep the signed original as well.
  • Very large merges of several hundred megabytes can run out of memory on a phone. A laptop handles them easily.

Merge PDFs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android

The page works the same in Safari, Chrome, Edge and Firefox. On a Mac, Preview can also merge by dragging thumbnails between windows, but it has no page ranges. On Windows there is no built-in way at all, so a browser tool is the simplest option. On iPhone and Android, choose the files from the Files app or your downloads; the merged PDF is saved where your browser saves downloads.

Questions and answers

How do I merge PDF files into one?
Add the files on this page, drag them into order and press Merge PDFs. The combined document is saved to your device. To leave out pages, type a range such as 1-3 in the pages box of that file.
Is it free to merge PDFs?
Yes. There is no account, no watermark and no daily limit. The work happens on your device, so there is no server cost to pass on.
Are my files uploaded?
No. The PDFs are read and combined in your browser and saved straight to your device. Nothing is sent to a server, which you can check by going offline after the page loads.
How many PDFs can I combine?
As many as your device can hold in memory. Dozens of ordinary documents are no problem; only merges of several hundred megabytes may be slow on a phone.
Can I merge only certain pages?
Yes. Type the pages you want in the box under each file, such as 1-3, 5. Or switch to page mode and drag single pages into place.
Can I combine PDF and JPG files?
Yes. Add images together with the PDFs. Each image becomes one page and can be dragged anywhere in the order.
Does merging reduce quality?
No. Pages are copied as they are, without converting them to images, so text, vector graphics and pictures look exactly as before.
What is the best way to merge PDFs on a Mac or Windows PC?
Use this page in any browser: it works the same on both and needs no install. On a Mac, Preview can merge too, but it cannot pick page ranges; Windows has no built-in merger.
