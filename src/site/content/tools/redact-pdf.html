How to redact a PDF

  1. Open your PDF

    The editor opens with the redaction tools ready. The file stays on your device.

  2. Mark what to remove

    Select text to black out, or draw a box over any area: a signature, a photo, a stamp.

  3. Apply the redactions

    Applying removes the marked content from the page. Until then a mark is only a plan and can still be moved or deleted.

  4. Download the checked file

    Before saving, the finished file is searched for every redacted phrase. You see the result, then the file is saved.

To redact a PDF is to remove information from it for good, so nobody who gets the file can read, copy or search for it. A black rectangle drawn on top is not enough: the words stay in the file under it. This tool removes them, runs in your browser so the document is never uploaded, and checks the finished file before you send it.

Black out text in a PDF permanently

Select the words you want gone, such as a name, an account number or an address, and they are marked for redaction. To find every place a word appears, use the search in the editor and mark each match. When you apply, the letters under the marks are taken out of the page and a solid black bar is drawn where they were. The rest of the text stays real text: it can still be selected, copied and searched.

Redact an area or an image

Draw a box to remove everything inside it: part of a scan, a photo, a signature, a QR code or a stamp. On a scanned page there is no text, only a picture of it, so drawing a box over the words is the way to redact a scan.

Check that the text is really gone

Before the file is saved, it is opened again the way any PDF reader would open it, and every phrase you redacted is looked for in the page text, the document properties and the bookmarks. If one is still found, the file is not downloaded and you are told which words to redact again. If nothing is found, you see a confirmation and the file is saved.

How it works

Redaction runs in a PDF engine inside your browser. Applying a mark removes the text and image content under it from the page, not just its appearance. Words you redacted are then also removed from places a PDF keeps copies of text: the title and other properties, bookmarks and attached files. Finally the check above runs on the finished file. The document never leaves your device.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Court filings and discovery, records requests, HR and medical documents, bank statements for a landlord or a visa portal, invoices with customer details, and anything shared outside the company.

  • A redaction cannot be undone in the saved file. Keep your original and send the redacted copy.
  • Encrypted PDFs are redacted on the page, but copies in their properties and bookmarks cannot be cleaned. Redact an unencrypted copy and add a password afterwards with Protect PDF.
  • Scans contain pictures of text, so the check cannot read them: redact scans with a box and look at the result.
  • Information can be guessed from context, such as the length of a name next to its first letter. Redact whole phrases.

Questions and answers

How can I redact a PDF for free?
Open the file on this page, select the text or draw a box over what to remove, apply the redactions and download. It is free, with no account and no watermark.
How do I black out text in a PDF?
Select the words with the redaction tool and apply. The words are removed and a black bar takes their place, so the text cannot be copied or found by search.
Is a black box the same as redaction?
No. A box drawn with a shape or highlight tool only covers the words, and anyone can copy them from under it. Redaction removes them from the file.
Can redacted text be recovered?
Not from a file redacted here: the words are taken out of the page and its properties, and the check confirms they cannot be found. Keep the original if you may need it.
Can I undo a redaction?
Before you apply, yes: move or delete the mark. After the file is saved, no. That is the point of redaction.
Does redaction remove metadata?
Words you redact are also removed from the title and other properties, bookmarks and attachments. To clear all properties, use Remove Metadata as well.
Is my document uploaded?
No. The PDF is opened, redacted and checked in your browser, and saved straight to your device.
What does redacted mean?
Redacted means private or sensitive parts of a document have been removed before it is shared, usually shown as black bars.
