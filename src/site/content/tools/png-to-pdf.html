How to convert PNG to PDF

  1. Add your PNG files

    Choose one image or many at once. You can keep adding files before converting.

  2. Pick the quality

    High keeps every pixel and transparency. Medium and low make smaller files for email.

  3. Convert and save

    Press Convert to PDF. You get one PDF with a page for each image, in the order you added them.

Converting PNG to PDF puts your images into a document: several screenshots become one file, they print page by page and open in any PDF reader. At high quality the pixels go in unchanged, so text in screenshots stays exactly as sharp. The conversion runs in your browser.

What is a PNG, and is it the same as a PDF?

PNG is an image format that keeps every pixel exactly, with no compression blur, and supports transparent backgrounds. That makes it the usual format for screenshots, logos and diagrams. A PDF is a document: it can hold many pages, real text, images and links. Turning PNGs into a PDF does not change the pictures; it puts each one on a page.

Does PNG to PDF reduce quality?

Not at high quality: the PNG is placed into the PDF as it is, without any loss, and transparent areas stay transparent. Medium and low scale large images down and save them as JPEG inside the PDF, which makes the file much smaller but replaces transparency with a solid background. Keep high quality for logos and graphics with transparent parts; use medium for big screenshots you want to email.

Screenshots from an iPhone or Android phone

Phone screenshots are PNG files. Open this page on the phone, tap the upload area and choose the screenshots from your photo library in the order you want them. The PDF is saved to Files on an iPhone or to Downloads on Android. To change the order afterwards or add PDF pages, use Merge PDF.

How it works

Each image becomes one page exactly the size of the picture, so nothing is cropped or stretched. At high quality the PNG is embedded losslessly; at medium and low it is resized and re-encoded in your browser first. The PDF is assembled in memory and saved to your device. No image is uploaded.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Sending a series of screenshots as evidence or a bug report, turning a chat or a booking into a document, collecting diagrams for a course, printing digital artwork, attaching scanned pages saved as PNG.

  • Photos in JPG have their own converter: JPG to PDF.
  • Searchable text is not created; screenshots stay pictures. Run OCR to make the text searchable.
  • Page size follows the image. For uniform A4 or Letter pages, run the PDF through Fix page size.
