How to convert MOBI to PDF

  1. Add your MOBI books

    One or several .mobi files. They stay on your device.

  2. Press Convert to PDF

    The first run downloads the converter engine, about 39 MB, once.

  3. Save the PDFs

    One book downloads as a PDF named after it; several come as a ZIP.

MOBI is the older Kindle eBook format. Converting MOBI to PDF gives you a book you can read on any computer, phone or tablet, print and search. It works with DRM-free books and runs in your browser, so your library is never uploaded.

Which books can be converted

Only DRM-free books. Most Kindle store purchases carry DRM and cannot be opened here. Personal documents, public domain books and titles from DRM-free stores convert normally. A file that fails is usually protected, damaged or renamed to .mobi without being a MOBI.

Only .mobi files

AZW, AZW3 and KFX files are not accepted. Convert them to MOBI or EPUB with another program first; EPUB files can then go through EPUB to PDF.

Pages, text and images

Headings, bold and italic text, lists and embedded images carry over. The text stays real text, so it is selectable and searchable. MOBI text reflows to the screen, so the PDF gets fixed pages of its own: page numbers will not match Kindle locations, and there is no setting for page or font size.

Several books at once

One file downloads as a PDF named after the book. Several are converted one after another and delivered as mobi-converted.zip with one PDF each.

How it works

A PDF engine (PyMuPDF compiled for the browser) reads the book and writes the PDF on your device. The first conversion downloads the engine, about 39 MB, once. The book is never sent to a server.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Reading an eBook on a computer, printing a chapter, sharing a book with someone without a Kindle, archiving a library in a universal format.

  • No font or page size setting. The engine lays the book out on pages of its own.
  • If a book will not convert but opens in Calibre, converting it there to a fresh MOBI usually works.

Questions and answers

How do I convert MOBI to PDF?
Add the .mobi file, press Convert to PDF and save the result.
Is MOBI to PDF free?
Yes, with no account, no watermark and no limit on books.
Can I convert Kindle store books?
Only if they are DRM-free. Most Kindle purchases have DRM and cannot be opened.
Does it accept AZW, AZW3 or KFX?
No, only .mobi. Convert other formats to MOBI or EPUB first.
Will the page count match my Kindle?
No. MOBI text reflows, so the PDF gets its own fixed pages.
Are images and formatting kept?
Headings, bold and italic, lists and images carry over, and the text stays searchable.
Can I convert several books at once?
Yes. One book gives a PDF; several are saved together as a ZIP.
Is my eBook uploaded?
No. The conversion runs in your browser and the book never leaves your device.
