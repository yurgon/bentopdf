How to convert JPG to PDF

  1. Add your images

    Choose one JPG or select many at once. Add more files any time before converting.

  2. Pick the quality

    High keeps every pixel. Medium and low shrink big photos so the PDF is easier to email.

  3. Convert and save

    Press Convert to PDF. One PDF with a page for each image is saved to your device.

What you get

What each quality setting does
Quality What happens to the image Best for
High Kept exactly as it is Printing, documents you will zoom into
Medium Longest side up to 2,500 px, JPEG quality 75% Email and upload forms
Low Longest side up to 1,500 px, JPEG quality 50% Strict size limits, previews

A JPG to PDF converter packs pictures into a document that opens the same way on every phone and computer, prints page by page and can be sent as one attachment. Here the conversion happens in your browser, so private photos such as passports or bank letters stay with you.

Which quality to choose

Phone cameras take photos of 12 megapixels and more, about 3 to 5 MB each. Ten of them at high quality make a PDF of 30 to 50 MB, too big for most email. Medium scales each photo to at most 2,500 pixels on the long side, still sharp enough to read and print on A4 or Letter, and the file is usually several times smaller. Low is for upload forms with strict limits; text stays readable on screen. Already have the PDF and it is still too big? Compress PDF squeezes it further.

Page size, orientation and order

Each page takes the shape of its picture: portrait photos make portrait pages, landscape photos landscape pages, and nothing is cropped. Pages follow the order in which you added the images, so add them in the order you want them to appear. To mix photos with other PDFs and drag them around, use Merge PDF, which accepts both. If a printer or a portal needs every page to be exactly A4 or Letter, run the result through Fix page size.

Photos from an iPhone or Android phone

Open this page on the phone, tap the upload area and choose Photo Library or Gallery. iPhones often save photos as HEIC rather than JPG; Safari usually converts them when you pick them, but if a photo is refused, use HEIC to PDF. The finished PDF lands in Files on iPhone or in Downloads on Android.

How it works

At high quality each JPG is placed into the PDF unchanged, so there is no second round of compression. At medium and low the image is first resized and re-saved in your browser, then placed on its page. The PDF is built in memory on your device and saved directly to it; no image is uploaded.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Sending photos of a signed form, turning receipts into one expense report, scanning homework with a phone, attaching ID copies to an application, keeping a recipe or a drawing as a document.

  • PNG, WebP or HEIC files need their own converters: PNG to PDF keeps transparency, the others are in the tools list.
  • Searchable text is not created; the pages are pictures. Run OCR on the PDF if you need to search or copy the text.
  • Very large batches of hundreds of full-size photos may be slow on a phone. Use medium quality, or convert in smaller groups and merge them.

Questions and answers

How do I convert or save a JPG as a PDF?
Add the images on this page, pick a quality and press Convert to PDF. A PDF with one image per page is saved to your device.
Can I combine multiple JPGs into one PDF?
Yes. Select all the images at once or keep adding them; they all go into one PDF, a page each, in the order you added them.
Will my image quality be affected?
Not at high quality: the JPG is placed into the PDF unchanged. Medium and low reduce size by scaling large photos down, which you only notice when zooming in.
Is it free to convert JPG to PDF?
Yes, with no sign up, no watermark and no limit on the number of images.
Are my photos uploaded?
No. The PDF is made in your browser and saved directly to your device. Nothing is sent to a server.
Why is my PDF so large?
Photos from modern phones are big. Choose medium quality, or run the finished PDF through Compress PDF.
Can I make every page A4 or Letter size?
Pages match the shape of each photo. To force a standard size, run the PDF through Fix page size afterwards.
How do I convert iPhone photos to PDF?
Open this page in Safari, tap the upload area and choose Photo Library. If your photos are HEIC and get refused, use HEIC to PDF instead.
