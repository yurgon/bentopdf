How to convert a PDF to text

  1. Add your PDFs

    One or several. They are read on your device.

  2. Extract the text

    The first run downloads the text engine once, about 39 MB. Then extraction takes seconds.

  3. Save the .txt

    One file is saved as .txt; several come together in a ZIP.

Converting a PDF to text gives you just the words, without layout, fonts or pictures, in a .txt file that opens anywhere. It is the quickest way to search a long document, quote from it, or feed it to another program. Here it runs in your browser, so your documents are never uploaded.

Extract text from several PDFs

Add a whole folder of reports, articles or statements. Each PDF becomes a .txt file with the same name, and they are saved together in a ZIP. The text follows the reading order of each page, page after page, in UTF-8, so accents and non-Latin alphabets come through.

Scanned PDFs need OCR

This tool reads the text that is stored inside a PDF. A scan or a photo saved as PDF only contains pictures of letters, so the result is empty. Quick test: if you cannot select a word in your PDF viewer, use PDF OCR, which recognizes the letters and also gives you a text file.

Why text will not copy from some PDFs

Copying from a PDF viewer often breaks lines in the middle of sentences, mixes columns or picks up headers and page numbers. Some PDFs block copying with a permissions password; those can be opened with Unlock PDF first. And in a few files the letters are drawn with fonts that have no proper character map, so the extracted text looks like gibberish; running OCR on such a file gives readable text.

How it works

Text is extracted with MuPDF, a PDF engine running in your browser. It reads the characters stored in each page together with their positions, puts them into reading order and writes them as plain text. The first time, the engine (about 39 MB) is downloaded and kept by your browser. Your files are never sent anywhere.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Searching and quoting long reports, collecting text for research or notes, pasting a contract into an email, importing documents into a translation or AI tool, and keeping plain-text archives.

  • Formatting is not kept: no bold, tables or pictures. For that, use PDF to Word.
  • Scans give no text without OCR.
  • Complex layouts such as tables and multi-column pages may come out in an unexpected order.

Questions and answers

How do I convert a PDF to text?
Add the PDF on this page and press Extract Text. A .txt file with all its text is saved to your device.
Does it keep the formatting?
No. You get plain text in reading order. For headings and tables, use PDF to Word.
Will it work on a scanned PDF?
No, a scan has no text inside. Use PDF OCR, which reads the letters and also gives you a text file.
How do I extract text from a PDF for free?
On this page it is free, with no account and no limit on files.
Why can't I copy text from my PDF?
It is either a scan, protected against copying, or made with fonts that hide the letters. Try PDF OCR or Unlock PDF.
Can I convert several PDFs at once?
Yes. Each becomes its own .txt file, saved together in a ZIP.
Is my file uploaded?
No. The text is extracted in your browser and your file never leaves your device.
