How to rotate a PDF

  1. Add your PDF

    One file. It stays on your device and every page shows as a thumbnail.

  2. Turn the pages

    Use the left and right buttons under a page, or the buttons at the top to turn every page at once.

  3. Apply and save

    Press Apply Rotations. The PDF downloads with the pages turned for good.

What you get

Clicks and rotation
Clicks Direction Result
1 Right Turned 90 degrees clockwise
2 Either way Turned 180 degrees, upside down
3 Right (or 1 left) Turned 270 degrees, 90 counterclockwise

To rotate a PDF is to turn its pages so they read the right way up, for example a scan that came out sideways. A viewer's own rotate button only changes what you see until you close the file. Here the rotation is written into the PDF, so it holds in every viewer and on paper. The file stays in your browser.

Rotate one page or all pages

Every page appears as a thumbnail with its own left and right buttons, so one sideways scan in a 40 page file can be fixed on its own. The Left and Right buttons under Batch Actions turn every page together when the whole document is the wrong way up.

Turn 90, 180 or 270 degrees

Rotations add up in 90 degree steps. Click the same direction twice for 180 degrees and three times for 270, which equals one click the other way. The thumbnail updates so you see the current state before saving.

The rotation is saved in the file

The tool changes each page's rotation setting instead of redrawing it, so text stays selectable, images keep their resolution and the size stays about the same. Every viewer and printer honors the setting, unlike the temporary rotate button of a viewer that resets when you close the file.

Other angles and page groups

This tool turns pages in 90 degree steps only. For other angles use Rotate by Custom Degrees, and Deskew PDF straightens a tilted scan automatically. There is no page range field here; for groups of pages use the PDF Multi Tool.

How it works

The thumbnails are drawn by your browser. When you press Apply Rotations, a PDF library on your device sets the rotation of each changed page and writes a new file under the original name. The document is never uploaded.

What people use it for, and when it will not work

Scans fed into the scanner sideways, phone photos turned into PDFs, landscape pages in a portrait document, forms that print upside down.

  • Bookmarks are not carried over, because the rotated file is built from copies of the pages. Links on pages, images and annotations come through.
  • Scans that are tilted, not turned, need Deskew PDF.
  • Fix the page size afterwards with Fix Page Size if a printer needs it.

Questions and answers

How do I rotate a PDF?
Add the file, turn the pages with the left and right buttons, press Apply Rotations and save.
Can I rotate just one page?
Yes. Each page thumbnail has its own buttons, so one page can be turned on its own.
How do I rotate a PDF 180 degrees?
Click the same direction twice. Rotations add up in 90 degree steps.
Will the rotation stay when I open the file elsewhere?
Yes. It is saved in the PDF itself, so every viewer and printer shows the pages turned.
Does rotating reduce quality?
No. The pages are not redrawn, so text stays selectable and images keep their resolution.
Can I rotate by an angle other than 90 degrees?
Not here. Use Rotate by Custom Degrees for any angle, or Deskew PDF to straighten a scan.
Does rotating keep my bookmarks?
No. Bookmarks are not carried over, though links, images and annotations are.
Is rotating a PDF free and private?
Yes. It is free, needs no account, and the file never leaves your device.
