Your files

When you choose a file, it is opened by code running in your browser tab. It is not uploaded to a server, not stored by us and not shared with anyone. When you close the tab, it is gone from memory. Some tools download a processing module the first time you use them, such as the office-document converter; that is code coming to your device, not your file going out.

What we count

To know which pages and tools are useful, the site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It counts visits without cookies and stores nothing that identifies you or your file.

We also use Google Analytics, which sets cookies. If you are in Europe, it starts only after you press Accept in the bar at the bottom of the page; elsewhere it runs when the page opens. Google Analytics sees which pages you open and general data about your device and region. It never sees your files.

What we do not do

  • No accounts and no sign-up.
  • No advertising.
  • Your files are never uploaded, counted or analysed.

Settings stored in your browser

If you switch between the light and dark theme, the choice is kept in your browser’s local storage so the page looks the same next time. It never leaves your device.
