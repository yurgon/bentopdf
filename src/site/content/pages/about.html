Who makes it

Plyleaf is an independent project, not a company product with a paid tier hidden behind the free one.

Why it is free

The tools run in your browser, so there are no servers that process files and no bill that grows with every visitor. Hosting a set of static pages costs very little, which is why there is no account, no daily limit, no watermark and no advertising.

Built on open source

Plyleaf is built on BentoPDF, an open-source PDF toolkit, and adds its own page design, explanations and tools such as the booklet maker. The full source code is public under the AGPL-3.0 licence: read it on GitHub.

How to check that files stay on your device

Open a tool, switch off your internet connection, then choose a file. The tool still works, because nothing is sent anywhere. The privacy page explains what the site does count.
