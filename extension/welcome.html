Plyleaf

Any web page, saved as a clean PDF

  • One click. Press the Plyleaf button, or right-click a page and choose Save page as PDF. Links and text stay selectable.
  • A PDF editor built in. Add text, draw, highlight, sign and fill in forms right in Chrome, then save. Works offline.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Pages and files are handled on your computer. No account, no limits.

Your PDF settings

You can change these any time in the Plyleaf button.

Orientation

Turn on page saving

Plyleaf saves pages with Chrome's own print engine. Chrome will ask you to allow this once. It is used only while a page is being saved, and the PDF goes straight to your Downloads folder.

Edit PDFs as soon as you open them

Open PDF links in the Plyleaf editor instead of Chrome's viewer. Chrome will ask to let Plyleaf read PDF links on any site.

Keep Plyleaf one click away

Click the puzzle icon at the top right of Chrome, then the pin next to Plyleaf.
